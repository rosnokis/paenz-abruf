As it gets colder outside and winter wraps the world in quiet tones, it becomes all the cozier indoors. During this magical time, we invite you to join us in listening to familiar and unfamiliar winter songs. Melodies become rays of sunshine and vibrant colors that enchant the gray days. Feel free to hum along, sing along, sway along, and join in the singing!
Sponsored by Kuratorium KölnMusik e. V.
