Die Filmreihe Cinemania Kalk zeigt den wundervollen DER EISKALTE ENGEL – LE SAMOURAÏ (1967) von Jean-Pierre Melville … “In a career-defining performance, Alain Delon plays Jef Costello, a contract killer with samurai instincts. An elegantly stylized masterpiece of cool by maverick director Jean‑Pierre Melville, LE SAMOURAÏ is a razor-sharp cocktail of 1940s American gangster cinema and 1960s French pop culture – with a liberal dose of Japanese lone-warrior mythology.
Alan Delon's performance as the stoic contract killer significantly contributed to the development of a minimalist aesthetic in gangster films... The film's serene ambiance, mirrored in its protagonist's unemotional demeanour, has left a profound impact on cinema; inspiring the likes of John Woo’s “The Killer“ (1989), Jim Jarmusch’s “Ghost Dog“ (1999) and Nicolas Winding Refn's “Drive“ (2011).“ Prince Charles Cinema
