English Theatre Düsseldorf (ETD) is delighted to bring Patrick Barlow’s British Christmas comedy THE MESSIAH to the Horizont Theater this December!
Patrick Barlow’s THE MESSIAH, is an amusing and farcical re-imagining of the greatest story of all time; The Nativity Story! The entire ensemble of… two, under the direction of “distinguished historian and thespian”, artistic director Jeffrey Thespus Wimpole (Mike McAlpine) and his incompetent, er, brave assistant Humphrey Dott (Bevan Thomson), bring us their version of the entire Christmas story, with the help of fantastic opera singer (Anna Moog), who brilliantly sings excerpts from Handel’s Messiah.
