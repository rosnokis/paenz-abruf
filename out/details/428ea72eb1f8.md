ENSEMBLE SOZUSINGEN
The head wants one thing, but the gut, hands, and feet want to dance, and the heart is leaping with excitement!
ENSEMBLE SOZUSINGEN takes you on a vocal roller coaster ride: newly arranged children’s songs, classical sounds, and creative imagery invite you to listen, move along – and reflect. A theatrical concert for children and everyone accompanying them.
