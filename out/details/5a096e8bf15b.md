A story by Sven Nordqvist, adapted by Tristan Berger
★ High up in the hayloft of an old farmhouse lives a family of elves: the 427-year-old grandfather, the elf parents and their curious children. It has snowed, dusk is falling outside and the scent of Christmas is in the air. But the elf mother is worried – will people forget their little helpers this year? A concert for families, featuring music by Tchaikovsky, Rimsky-Korsakov, Grieg, Saint-Saëns and Järnefelt.
