Studierende der Hochschule für Musik und Tanz Köln | Johanna Risse | Corinna Vogel
What catchy tune will be on our minds this summer? We don’t know yet, but we’re already excited… We’re just as excitedly awaiting the final Veedel concerts of the season: students from the Cologne University of Music and Dance are collaborating on a concert program created especially for PhilharmonieVeedel. From the theme to the music to the staging, everything is in the students’ hands. So we can look forward to the concerts with great anticipation. »The stage is set« for a special surprise!
Sponsored by Kuratorium KölnMusik e. V.
